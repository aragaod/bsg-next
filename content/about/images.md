Each image on the home page shows one molecule studied with several techniques, all at UK national facilities: bio-SAXS on beamline B21 and X-ray crystallography at Diamond Light Source, and cryo-EM at the Electron Bio-Imaging Centre (eBIC). A different image is shown each day.

The images were prepared for the BSG from public data (SASBDB, EMDB and PDB) and the published papers. We thank the authors for making their data openly available.
