Early-career structural biologists often need advice from outside their own lab: on techniques, careers, fellowships or moving between academia, facilities and industry. The committee is developing three ideas.

## Virtual coffee shop

Informal online drop-in sessions where anyone can chat with experienced members from academia, national facilities and industry. No preparation needed: bring a question or just listen.

## Flash mentoring

One-off conversations matched to a specific question, such as "how do I start with cryo-EM?", "what is it like to work at a facility?" or "how do I prepare a fellowship application?". A single meeting, with no long-term commitment on either side.

## Secondment award

Support for an early-career researcher to spend time in another lab or at a facility to learn a new technique, bringing integrative structural biology into their own work.

*These schemes are proposals and their details are still to be agreed. If you would like to volunteer as a mentor, please contact the BSG Secretary.*
