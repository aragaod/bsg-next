The committee is considering a pilot online chat space for the BSG community, most likely on Discord, to complement the Winter Meeting and the BCA Spring Meeting.

## What it could be for

- Asking and answering practical questions, from sample preparation to data processing
- Sharing job adverts, courses, papers and new structures
- Finding collaborators and people with access to particular methods or facilities
- Keeping conversations going before and after meetings

## How it would work

- **Channels by topic and technique**, for example crystallography, cryo-EM, SAXS, imaging, modelling and AI, careers and meetings.
- **Open to all** BSG and BCA members, and to others working in structural biology.
- **Moderated by volunteers** from the committee and the community, following a short code of conduct: be respectful and inclusive, share openly but never confidential or unpublished data belonging to others, and no advertising beyond relevant jobs and events.
- **Your choice to join.** The chat would run on an external platform with its own terms and privacy policy; this website would only link to it and would not embed it.

*This is a proposal. If the pilot goes ahead, an invitation link will appear here.*
