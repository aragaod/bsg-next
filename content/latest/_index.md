---
title: "New UK structures"
description: "Structures released in the last few weeks from UK-funded research or with data collected at UK facilities, updated automatically every week."
---

This list is compiled automatically every week from public databases. It includes new entries in the [Protein Data Bank](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pdbe/) that were funded by UK organisations or used data collected at Diamond Light Source (cryo-EM entries link to their [EMDB](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/emdb/) maps), and new [SASBDB](https://www.sasbdb.org/) entries measured at UK facilities. Inclusion does not imply involvement of the BSG.
