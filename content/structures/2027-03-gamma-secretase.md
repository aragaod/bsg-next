γ-Secretase is an enzyme complex that cuts other proteins inside the cell membrane; one of its products is the amyloid-β peptide that accumulates in the brain in Alzheimer's disease. Complexes like this are very hard to crystallise. In 2015 Xiao-chen Bai, Sjors Scheres and colleagues at the MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology, working with Yigong Shi's group at Tsinghua University, used cryo-EM to obtain a 3.4 Å structure of the human complex. It was an early showcase of the 'resolution revolution', when new detectors and image-processing software, including Scheres's RELION, brought cryo-EM to atomic detail.
