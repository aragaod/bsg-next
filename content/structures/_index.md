title: "Structure of the month"
description: "A structure from UK structural biology each month, told in plain language, rotating across techniques."
---

Each month the committee picks a structure from UK structural biology, old or new, and explains it in about 100 words. Entries are scheduled in advance and appear on the first day of their month.
