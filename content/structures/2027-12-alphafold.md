Predicting a protein's structure from its amino-acid sequence was a challenge for half a century. In 2020 AlphaFold 2, developed by John Jumper, Demis Hassabis and colleagues at DeepMind in London, predicted structures in the CASP14 assessment with accuracy often competitive with experiment. With EMBL-EBI at Hinxton, DeepMind then released predictions for over 200 million proteins in the AlphaFold Protein Structure Database. Hassabis and Jumper shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. Predictions now work alongside experiment: they help solve crystal structures by molecular replacement, fit cryo-EM maps and guide new experiments.
