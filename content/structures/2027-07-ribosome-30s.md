The ribosome reads messenger RNA and builds proteins, and many antibiotics work by jamming it. In 2000 Venki Ramakrishnan's group at the MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology solved the structure of the small (30S) ribosomal subunit from the bacterium Thermus thermophilus at about 3 Å resolution. They went on to show how antibiotics bind to it and how it checks that each codon is paired with the right transfer RNA. Ramakrishnan shared the 2009 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Thomas Steitz and Ada Yonath for studies of the structure and function of the ribosome.
