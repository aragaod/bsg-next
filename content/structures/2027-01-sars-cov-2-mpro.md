Early in the COVID-19 pandemic, a team at Diamond Light Source and the University of Oxford ran a rapid crystallographic fragment screen against the SARS-CoV-2 main protease, an enzyme the virus needs to copy itself. They soaked well over a thousand small chemical fragments into crystals of the enzyme, found dozens bound in its active site, and made the results public straight away. These starting points seeded the COVID Moonshot, an open-science, crowdsourced drug discovery project, and show how synchrotron fragment screening can start drug design at speed.
