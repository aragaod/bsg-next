The BSG will run sessions at the BCA Spring Meeting 2027 on the theme *AI in the era of large-scale structural biology*. The BCA Spring Meeting 2027, *Diversity in Diffraction*, takes place in person at the University of York from Monday 22 to Wednesday 24 March 2027.

## Registration and abstracts

**[Registration is open](https://registrations.hg3conferences.co.uk/hg3/frontend/reg/thome.csp?pageID=156758&eventID=380)** on the conference website, which also has the venue, accommodation and the draft programme.

- **18 January 2027:** deadline for abstracts (submission opens shortly)
- **February 2027:** notification of abstract acceptance
- **26 February 2027, 23:59:** early bird registration closes
- **18 March 2027, 23:59:** registration closes

Questions about registration: [events@hg3.co.uk](mailto:events@hg3.co.uk).

## Provisional sessions

1. Engineering biology: protein design and molecular modelling
2. Beyond static structures: protein dynamics
3. Seeing the bigger picture: whole viral and cellular structures
4. Joint session with the Early Stage Crystallographers Group
5. Open session

*Session titles are provisional and may change.*
