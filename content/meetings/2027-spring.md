The BSG will run sessions at the BCA Spring Meeting 2027 on the theme *AI in the era of large-scale structural biology*. Dates and venue will be announced by the [BCA](https://crystallography.org.uk/spring-meetings/).

## Provisional sessions

1. Engineering biology: protein design and molecular modelling
2. Beyond static structures: protein dynamics
3. Seeing the bigger picture: whole viral and cellular structures
4. Joint session with the Early Stage Crystallographers Group
5. Open session

*Session titles are provisional and may change.*
