---
title: "The BSG has a new website"
date: 2026-10-06
author: "David Aragao"
summary: "biostructures.org.uk is now the BSG's home on the web, covering the full range of structural biology techniques our members use."
---

The BSG committee has approved biostructures.org.uk as the group's new website. It replaces bsg.crystallography.org.uk, which will point visitors here; programmes from past meetings remain available in the [meetings archive](/meetings/#archive).

The new site broadens the group's home on the web from crystallography to the full range of techniques our members use: cryo-EM, bio-SAXS, NMR, mass spectrometry, imaging, modelling and AI, and structure-based drug design. It adds an [events calendar](/events/) you can subscribe to, [training](/training/) listings, a [structure of the month](/structures/) and a [resources](/resources/) hub organised by technique, and committee members can update it without editing HTML.

Suggestions for events, courses, structures or news are welcome: please contact the BSG web officer or the Secretary.
