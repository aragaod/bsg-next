Registration is open for the [BSG Winter Meeting 2026](/meetings/2026-winter/), *Capturing Protein Motion: Structural Biology of Dynamic and Disordered Proteins*, on Friday 11 December 2026 at the Leicester Institute of Structural and Chemical Biology, University of Leicester.

Speakers are John Christodoulou (UCL), Rebecca Beveridge (Strathclyde), Sarah Rouse (Imperial), Cyril Dominguez (Leicester), and Matthew Higgins (Oxford), who gives the keynote lecture. Several short talks will be selected from poster abstracts, and poster prizes will be awarded.

- Registration: £80, limited to 120 places, closes 26 November 2026
- Short-talk abstracts: by 20 November 2026

**[Register and submit an abstract](https://registrations.hg3conferences.co.uk/hg3/frontend/reg/thome.csp?pageID=161036&eventID=388)**
