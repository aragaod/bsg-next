Registration is open for the [CCP4 Study Weekend 2027](https://studyweekend.ccp4.ac.uk/), *Structural Biology on the Move*, from Wednesday 6 to Friday 8 January 2027 at the East Midlands Conference Centre, Nottingham. This year's theme is time-resolved crystallography, with sessions on metalloproteins, serial crystallography data processing, refinement of ensembles and partial states, and complementary computational methods. The Diamond MX User Meeting runs on 6 January, before the Study Weekend opens that evening.

The Study Weekend is one of the best ways for students and early-career researchers to learn current methods from the people who develop them. **Students who register in person by 15 November get a bursary covering the registration fee and one night in halls**, so they only pay for any extra nights (or a subsidised £30 for one hotel night instead). Anyone can follow online for free, and students can present their work at the poster session on the evening of 6 January.

- Early bird registration, and the deadline for the student bursary: 15 November 2026
- In-person registration closes: 6 December 2026 (or earlier if places fill)
- Online registration: open until the meeting
- Travel bursaries for students and young postdocs from non-UK laboratories: apply by 31 October 2026

Bursaries are also available for active retired and emeritus scientists: see the [bursary details](https://studyweekend.ccp4.ac.uk/bursaries/).

**[Register on the Study Weekend website](https://studyweekend.ccp4.ac.uk/)**

The Study Weekend is also in our [events calendar](https://biostructures.org.uk/events/), which you can add to Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar.
