Registration is open for the BCA Spring Meeting 2027, *Diversity in Diffraction*, in person at the University of York from Monday 22 to Wednesday 24 March 2027. The BSG will run sessions on the theme *AI in the era of large-scale structural biology*: see the [BSG sessions page](/meetings/2027-spring/).

- Abstracts: submission opens shortly; deadline 18 January 2027
- Early bird registration: until 26 February 2027
- Registration closes: 18 March 2027

**[Register on the conference website](https://registrations.hg3conferences.co.uk/hg3/frontend/reg/thome.csp?pageID=156758&eventID=380)**
