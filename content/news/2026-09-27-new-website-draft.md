A draft of a new website for the Biological Structures Group is being prepared for review by the BSG committee and BCA council.

The new site broadens the group's home on the web from crystallography to the full range of techniques our members use: cryo-EM, bio-SAXS, imaging, modelling and AI, and structure-based drug design. It adds an events calendar, training listings, a structure of the month and community pages, and it can be updated by committee members without editing HTML.

Comments and suggestions are welcome through the committee.
