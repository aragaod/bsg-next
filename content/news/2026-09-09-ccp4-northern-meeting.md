Registration and abstract submission are open for the [CCP4 Northern Structural Biology Meeting 2026](https://nusbf.ncl.ac.uk/ccp4-northern-Meeting-2026/), from Wednesday 11 to Friday 13 November 2026 in the Hadrian Building, Newcastle University. This year the meeting has expanded to three days to make room for more speakers, and the keynote speaker is Professor Laura Spagnolo (University of Glasgow).

The meeting is free to attend and is a great opportunity for students and early-career researchers to present their work, share ideas and meet others in the structural biology community. There are prizes for the best talks and posters, and a limited number of travel bursaries for students presenting a talk.

- Abstract submission: by 18 October 2026 (extended from 12 October)
- Registration: by 30 October 2026

**[Register and submit an abstract](https://nusbf.ncl.ac.uk/ccp4-northern-Meeting-2026/)**
